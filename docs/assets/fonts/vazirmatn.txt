Vazirmatn 33.003, Regular (400) and SemiBold (600), cut down to the Arabic-script
characters (U+0600–06FF, U+0750–077F, U+08A0–08FF, U+FB50–FDFF, U+FE70–FEFF, ZWNJ/ZWJ
and the direction marks) with `pyftsubset --flavor=woff2`, so Latin text keeps the
theme's font. Used by `docs/assets/extra.css`.

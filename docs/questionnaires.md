# Questionnaires

The original HIES questionnaire forms as published by the Statistical Centre of
Iran, one PDF per year (1363–1367 have separate urban and rural forms). The
forms from 1383 on have a text layer and are extracted into the codebook (1382 has
one too, but its older 3+2 digit code scheme is left out); in the
[explorer](explorer.md), clicking a year in an item's timeline
opens the page it is printed on, with the row highlighted. The
earlier forms are scanned images, kept here for reference until they are read
with OCR.

For each extracted year there is also an Excel workbook of the corrected
expenditure items: the form's sections, group headers and items in order, a
sheet of the items alone with the groups each is printed under, and what
every column means. It is made from the same
[CSV](data.md#csv-columns) and keeps the codes as text, with their leading zero.
